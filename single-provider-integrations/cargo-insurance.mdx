## Introduction
This guide walks you through the Cargo Insurance APIs. They cover goods in transit by sea, air or road for a business, in two ways:

- **Single shipment**: quote one shipment, pay for it, and get its certificate.
- **Open cover**: a one-year policy for a business that ships regularly. The business pays a deposit once, then declares each shipment under the policy and gets a certificate for it straight away.

Both start from the same customer record and end on the same policy object.

### Basic Journey
1. **Authorize**: Get an access token to authenticate all API requests.
2. **Get the options**: Fetch the values your forms offer: goods categories, transport modes, coverages and the rest.
3. **Register the customer**: Register the business by its commercial registration (CR) number.
4. **Get a decision**: [Quote a shipment](#quote-a-shipment), or [apply for open cover](#apply-for-open-cover). The answer is approved, referred or rejected.
5. **Issue the policy**: Accept an approved quote. The policy comes back with a payment link.
6. **Payment**: The customer pays, and the policy is issued with its certificate and tax invoice.
7. **Declare shipments** (open cover only): Declare each shipment under the policy and get its certificate.

## Options

The [Get the options API](/cargo-api-reference/options/get-the-options) returns every list of values the other endpoints accept, each entry with the `value` to send and a `label` to show:

`customer_roles`, `transport_modes`, `goods_categories`, `packaging`, `conditions`, `characteristics`, `coverages`, `extensions`, `valuation_bases`, `incoterms`, `insurance_arranged_by`, `currencies` and `countries`.

Build your dropdowns from this response instead of hard-coding them, so a new value reaches your users without a release on your side.

| `coverages` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `icc_a` | All risks (Institute Cargo Clauses A) |
| `icc_b` | Named perils (Institute Cargo Clauses B) |
| `icc_c` | Basic perils (Institute Cargo Clauses C) |

## Customers

### Register a customer

The customer is the business the cargo is insured for. The [Register a customer API](/cargo-api-reference/customers/register-a-customer) takes its 10-digit CR number, its role in the trade and a contact person:

```json
{
  "cr_number": "1010456789",
  "role": "importer",
  "vat_number": "310123456700003",
  "contact": {
    "name": "Sara Alharbi",
    "email": "sara@example.com",
    "mobile": "+966500000001"
  }
}
```

The CR number is checked against the commercial registry:

- **The registry knows the business.** Its legal name and address come from the registry, and the customer's `verification.status` is `verified`. You do not need to ask your user for them.
- **The registry has no record.** The call answers `400` with code `40031`. Ask your user for the legal name and address and send the call again with `legal_name` and `address` (`street` and `city` at least). The customer is kept as entered, with `verification.status` `unverified`.

Registering a CR number you already registered updates that customer and answers `200` instead of `201`. Keep the customer's `id`: every quote and application names it as `customer_id`.

## Single shipment

### Quote a shipment

The [Quote a shipment API](/cargo-api-reference/single-shipment/quote-a-shipment) prices one shipment. It needs what sets the price: the goods, the route, the value and the cover wanted.

```json
{
  "customer_id": 2,
  "shipment": {
    "goods": {
      "description": "Consumer electronics, 400 cartons",
      "category": "electronics",
      "packaging": "cartons",
      "quantity": 400,
      "characteristics": ["fragile"]
    },
    "transport_mode": "sea",
    "origin": { "country": "CN", "city": "Shanghai" },
    "destination": { "country": "SA", "city": "Jeddah" },
    "invoice_value": 250000,
    "currency": "USD",
    "freight": 12000,
    "other_costs": 1500,
    "incoterm": "FOB",
    "coverage": "icc_a",
    "extensions": ["war", "strikes"]
  }
}
```

**The insured amount.** Leave `insured_amount` out and the shipment is insured for its value on the `valuation_basis`:

| `valuation_basis` | The shipment is valued at |
| --- | --- |
| `invoice_value` | `invoice_value` |
| `cif` | `invoice_value` + `freight` + `other_costs` |
| `cif_plus_10` (default) | `cif` plus 10% |

The invoice can be in any of the `currencies`. The price is always in SAR.

### Approved, referred or rejected

The call always answers `201`. What happened is in `status`:

| `status` | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `approved` | `quote` holds the price, valid until `quote.valid_until`. | Show the price and [issue the policy](#issue-the-policy). |
| `referred` | An underwriter has to review the request. `quote` is `null` and `reasons` says why. | Tell the customer it is under review, and [read the request](/cargo-api-reference/single-shipment/read-a-quote-request) again until the status changes. |
| `rejected` | The shipment cannot be insured. `reasons` says why. | Show the reasons. Nothing can be issued from this request. |

`reasons` are plain sentences you can show as they are. A referral that ends well turns `approved` and its `quote` is filled; its `reasons` stay, so you can still say why it was reviewed.

An approved quote looks like this:

```json
{
  "id": "a34af107-128e-4c87-934f-5658d04f5870",
  "type": "single_shipment",
  "status": "approved",
  "customer_id": 2,
  "shipment": { "…": "as you sent it" },
  "quote": {
    "insurance_provider": "walaa",
    "insurance_provider_name": "Walaa Cooperative Insurance",
    "coverage": "icc_a",
    "extensions": ["war", "strikes"],
    "valuation_basis": "cif_plus_10",
    "insured_amount": 289850,
    "currency": "USD",
    "insured_amount_sar": 1086937.5,
    "rate_percent": 0.45,
    "premium": 4891.22,
    "vat": 733.68,
    "total_price": 5624.9,
    "price_currency": "SAR",
    "valid_until": "2026-12-23"
  },
  "reasons": [],
  "missing_to_issue": [
    "departure_date",
    "conveyance.carrier_name",
    "references.invoice_number"
  ],
  "policy_id": null
}
```

`total_price` is what the customer pays, VAT included.

### What is needed to issue

A shipment can be quoted before everything about it is known. Three details are needed before its policy can be issued, though none of them changes the price:

- `departure_date`
- `conveyance.carrier_name`
- `references.invoice_number`

Send them with the quote request if you have them, or later with [Issue a policy](#issue-the-policy). `missing_to_issue` on the quote request lists the ones still missing.

## Open cover

### Apply for open cover

The [Apply for open cover API](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/apply-for-open-cover) asks for a one-year policy. Instead of one shipment, the application describes the business's trade: the goods it ships, its routes, the value it expects to ship in the year and the most any one shipment will be worth.

```json
{
  "customer_id": 2,
  "policy_period": { "start_date": "2026-12-03" },
  "goods": [
    {
      "description": "Consumer electronics",
      "category": "electronics",
      "packaging": "cartons",
      "characteristics": ["fragile"]
    }
  ],
  "estimated_annual_value": { "import": 12000000 },
  "currency": "SAR",
  "routes": [
    { "origin_country": "CN", "destination_country": "SA", "transport_mode": "sea" }
  ],
  "max_value_per_shipment": 1500000,
  "coverage": "icc_a",
  "extensions": ["war"],
  "insurance_history": { "first_policy": true }
}
```

The answer has the same three statuses as a single shipment; follow a referral with [Read an open-cover application](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/read-an-open-cover-application). When it is `approved`, `quote` holds the terms of the cover:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `cover_start`, `cover_end` | The policy period: one year from `policy_period.start_date`. |
| `declaration_rate_percent` | The rate every declared shipment is charged at, as a percentage of its insured amount. |
| `max_value_per_shipment` | The limit per shipment. A declared shipment above it is referred. |
| `estimated_annual_premium` | What the estimated annual value would cost at that rate, in SAR. |
| `deposit_premium` | The part of it paid up front to start the policy. |
| `premium`, `vat`, `total_price` | The deposit, its VAT, and what the customer pays now. |

## Issue the policy

The [Issue a policy API](/cargo-api-reference/policies/issue-a-policy) accepts an approved quote, for a single shipment or for open cover:

```json
{
  "quote_request_id": "a34af107-128e-4c87-934f-5658d04f5870",
  "redirect_url": "https://partner.example.com/cargo/policies/policyID",
  "shipment": {
    "departure_date": "2026-12-03",
    "arrival_date": "2026-12-28",
    "conveyance": {
      "carrier_name": "Bahri Logistics",
      "vessel_name": "MV Example",
      "voyage_or_flight_number": "V123"
    },
    "references": {
      "invoice_number": "INV-2026-0142",
      "bill_of_lading": "BL-778812"
    }
  }
}
```

- `shipment` is for a single shipment only, and carries the [details that were not on the quote request](#what-is-needed-to-issue). While any is missing the call answers `40033`, and `missing_fields` names them. An open cover needs only `quote_request_id`.
- `redirect_url` is where the customer is sent after paying. `policyID` anywhere in it is replaced with the policy's id.

The policy comes back **unpaid**: `status` is `0`, `coverage_confirmed` is `false` and `payment_link` is where the customer pays. Nothing is covered yet.

Calling Issue a policy again for the same quote before it is paid returns the same policy, updated. Once it is paid, the call answers `40026` with that `policy_id`.

### Payment

Send the customer to the policy's `payment_link`. It opens Yasmina's secure card page, which shows the premium, the VAT and the total.

- The link is valid for 24 hours. [Read the policy](/cargo-api-reference/policies/read-a-policy) to get the current one; it is renewed when it is about to expire.
- When the payment succeeds, the policy is issued and the customer is sent to your `redirect_url`.
- A failed or abandoned payment is never an API error. The policy stays unpaid until a payment succeeds.

### The issued policy

After payment, [Read the policy](/cargo-api-reference/policies/read-a-policy):

```json
{
  "id": 4,
  "type": "single_shipment",
  "status": 1,
  "coverage_confirmed": true,
  "payment_link": null,
  "price": 5624.9,
  "start_date": "2026-12-03",
  "provider_policy_id": "WAL/GIT/8854487",
  "policy_url": "https://sandbox.yasmina.ai/storage/generated-pdfs/cargo/policies/….pdf",
  "invoice_url": "https://sandbox.yasmina.ai/storage/generated-pdfs/cargo/invoices/….pdf",
  "purchased_at": "2026-11-23T08:40:12.000000Z",
  "meta_data": { "…": "the customer, the shipment and the quote the policy was issued from" }
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `coverage_confirmed` | `true` once the policy is paid for and issued. **Tell the customer the cargo is covered only when this is `true`.** |
| `provider_policy_id` | The insurance company's policy number. |
| `policy_url` | The policy certificate (PDF). |
| `invoice_url` | The tax invoice (PDF). |
| `payment_link` | `null` once the policy is issued. |

The [List policies API](/cargo-api-reference/policies/list-policies) returns all your cargo policies, and filters by `type` (`single_shipment` or `open_cover`) and `customer_id`.

## Declare shipments under an open cover

Once an open-cover policy is paid for and issued, the business declares each shipment with the [Declare a shipment API](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/declare-a-shipment) (`POST /policies/{id}/declarations`).

A declared shipment looks like the shipment of a quote request, with two differences:

- It does not name `coverage`, `extensions` or `valuation_basis`. Those are the policy's.
- It is covered straight away, so `departure_date`, `conveyance.carrier_name` and `references.invoice_number` are required, and `departure_date` has to fall within the policy period.

```json
{
  "shipment": {
    "goods": {
      "description": "400 cartons of tablets",
      "category": "electronics",
      "packaging": "container_fcl",
      "quantity": 400
    },
    "transport_mode": "sea",
    "origin": { "country": "CN", "city": "Shanghai" },
    "destination": { "country": "SA", "city": "Jeddah" },
    "invoice_value": 620000,
    "currency": "SAR",
    "freight": 14000,
    "departure_date": "2026-12-02",
    "conveyance": { "carrier_name": "Bahri Logistics", "vessel_name": "MV Jeddah Star" },
    "references": { "invoice_number": "INV-2026-0142", "bill_of_lading": "BHLU40112233" }
  }
}
```

The declaration's `status` says what happened:

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `issued` | The shipment is inside the policy's terms and is covered. `certificate_number` and `certificate_url` are its certificate. |
| `referred` | The shipment is outside the policy's terms: above the limit per shipment, goods or a route the policy was not written for, or hazardous goods. `reasons` says which. [Read the declaration](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/read-a-declaration) again until the status changes. |
| `rejected` | The shipment cannot be covered. `reasons` says why. |

`premium` is what the shipment costs at the policy's `declaration_rate_percent`. Declared premiums are reported on each declaration and totalled on the policy under `declarations` (`count`, `insured_amount_sar`, `premium`); they are not paid through a payment link.

Declaring under a policy that is not paid for yet, or is past its last day, answers `40027`.

## Testing in Sandbox

In Sandbox the commercial registry and the insurance company are simulated, so you can reach every branch of the journey on purpose. Prices, exchange rates and documents are samples and carry no cover.

| To get | Send |
| --- | --- |
| A verified customer | Any 10-digit `cr_number` that does not end in `000`. The legal name and address are sample values. |
| `40031`, the registry has no record | A `cr_number` ending in `000`, without `legal_name` and `address`. |
| An approved quote | A shipment with none of the conditions below. |
| A referred quote | `hazardous` among `goods.characteristics`, an insured amount above 5,000,000 SAR, or an `insured_amount` above the shipment's value plus 10%. |
| A rejected quote | `KP` as the origin or destination country, or `arms_and_ammunition` as the goods category. |
| A referred open-cover application | Hazardous goods, an estimated annual value above 100,000,000 SAR, a `max_value_per_shipment` above 5,000,000 SAR, or three or more past claims. |
| A referred declaration | A shipment above the policy's limit per shipment, a goods category or a route that is not on the policy, or hazardous goods. |

A referral is decided about 20 seconds after it was made: read the request or the declaration again after that and it is approved.

## Errors

| Endpoint | Code | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /cargo/customers` | `40031` | The commercial registry has no record of the CR number, and `legal_name` or `address` is missing. |
| `POST /cargo/policies` | `40032` | The quote request or application is referred or rejected. |
| | `40030` | The quote is past `valid_until`. Ask for a new one. |
| | `40033` | A single shipment still lacks details. `missing_fields` lists them. |
| | `40026` | The quote was already issued and paid for. `policy_id` is that policy. |
| `POST /cargo/policies/{id}/declarations` | `40027` | The open-cover policy is not paid for and issued, or is past its last day. |

A field that is missing or not valid answers `422` with code `42201`, and `details` names each field. The [Errors](/api-resources/errors#cargo-insurance) page has every code.
