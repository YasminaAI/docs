## Introduction
This guide walks you through the Cargo Insurance APIs. They cover a business's goods in transit by sea, air or land under a **marine open cover**: a one-year policy for a business that ships regularly. The business answers the insurance company's questions once, pays a deposit to start the policy, then declares each shipment under it and gets a certificate for it straight away.

### Basic Journey
1. **Authorize**: Get an access token to authenticate all API requests.
2. **Get the options**: Fetch the values your forms offer: transport modes, currencies, countries and the rest.
3. **Register the customer**: Register the business by its commercial registration (CR) number.
4. **Apply for open cover**: Send the business's answers. The decision is approved, referred or rejected.
5. **Issue the policy**: Accept the approved quote. The policy comes back with a payment link.
6. **Payment**: The customer pays the deposit, and the policy is issued with its schedule and tax invoice.
7. **Declare shipments**: Declare each shipment under the policy and get its certificate.

## Prerequisites
1. **Account in Yasmina** - See the <a href="/introduction#onboarding" target="_blank">Onboarding section</a> for details.
2. **Authenticated Token** - Follow the <a href="/authentication" target="_blank">Authentication guide</a> to generate one.
3. **Backend server** - Required to securely call Yasmina APIs.

Every path below is under `/api/v1/cargo`.

## Options

The [Get the options API](/cargo-api-reference/options/get-the-options) returns every list of values the other endpoints accept, each entry with the `value` to send and a `label` to show:

`customer_roles`, `transport_modes`, `goods_categories`, `packaging`, `conditions`, `characteristics`, `incoterms`, `insurance_arranged_by`, `currencies` and `countries`.

Build your dropdowns from this response instead of hard-coding them, so a new value reaches your users without a release on your side.

## Customers

### Register a customer

The customer is the business the cargo is insured for. The [Register a customer API](/cargo-api-reference/customers/register-a-customer) takes its 10-digit CR number, its role in the trade and a contact person:

```json
{
  "cr_number": "1010456789",
  "role": "importer",
  "vat_number": "310123456700003",
  "contact": {
    "name": "Sara Alharbi",
    "email": "sara@example.com",
    "mobile": "+966500000001"
  }
}
```

The CR number is checked against the commercial registry:

- **The registry knows the business.** Its legal name and address come from the registry, and the customer's `verification.status` is `verified`. You do not need to ask your user for them.
- **The registry has no record.** The call answers `400` with code `40031`. Ask your user for the legal name and address and send the call again with `legal_name` and `address` (`street` and `city` at least). The customer is kept as entered, with `verification.status` `unverified`.

Registering a CR number you already registered updates that customer and answers `200` instead of `201`. Keep the customer's `id`: every application names it as `customer_id`.

## Apply for open cover

The [Apply for open cover API](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/apply-for-open-cover) asks for the policy. It carries the business's answers to the insurance company's questions:

| Question | Field | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does the business do? | `business_activities` | Text |
| How much will it ship in the year? | `estimated_annual_value` | An amount, in `currency` |
| What goods are to be insured? | `goods_description` | Text, as detailed as the customer can make it |
| Where do the goods come from? | `origin_countries` | A list of `countries` values |
| Where do they go? | `destination_countries` | A list of `countries` values |
| How do they travel? | `transport_modes` | A list of `sea`, `air`, `land` |
| How many shipments in the year? | `estimated_annual_shipments` | A whole number |
| What is the most one shipment will be worth? | `max_value_per_shipment` | An amount, in `currency` |
| What claims were made in the last five years? | `claims_history` | A list of `year`, `amount` and an optional `description`; an empty list when there were none |

Every question has to be answered. `currency` is one of the `currencies` values and applies to every amount in the application.

```json
{
  "customer_id": 2,
  "business_activities": "Import and wholesale of consumer electronics",
  "estimated_annual_value": 20000000,
  "currency": "SAR",
  "goods_description": "Televisions, monitors and home audio, new, in cartons in full containers",
  "origin_countries": ["CN"],
  "destination_countries": ["SA"],
  "transport_modes": ["sea"],
  "estimated_annual_shipments": 40,
  "max_value_per_shipment": 1500000,
  "claims_history": [
    { "year": 2024, "amount": 42000, "description": "Water damage to one container" }
  ]
}
```

### Approved, referred or rejected

The call always answers `201`. What happened is in `status`:

| `status` | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `approved` | `quote` holds the terms, valid until `quote.valid_until`. | Show the terms and [issue the policy](#issue-the-policy). |
| `referred` | An underwriter has to review the application. `quote` is `null` and `reasons` says why. | Tell the customer it is under review, and [read the application](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/read-an-open-cover-application) again until the status changes. |
| `rejected` | The business cannot be insured. `reasons` says why. | Show the reasons. Nothing can be issued from this application. |

`reasons` are plain sentences you can show as they are. A referral that ends well turns `approved` and its `quote` is filled; its `reasons` stay, so you can still say why it was reviewed.

An approved application looks like this:

```json
{
  "id": "a34cf832-34e2-41a9-971f-46d2a0da3f57",
  "status": "approved",
  "customer_id": 2,
  "application": { "…": "as you sent it" },
  "quote": {
    "insurance_provider": "walaa",
    "insurance_provider_name": "Walaa Cooperative Insurance",
    "valuation_basis": "cif_plus_10",
    "estimated_annual_value_sar": 20000000,
    "max_value_per_shipment": 1500000,
    "currency": "SAR",
    "max_value_per_shipment_sar": 1500000,
    "declaration_rate_percent": 0.3,
    "estimated_annual_premium": 60000,
    "deposit_premium": 15000,
    "premium": 15000,
    "vat": 2250,
    "total_price": 17250,
    "price_currency": "SAR",
    "valid_until": "2026-12-24"
  },
  "reasons": [],
  "policy_id": null
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `declaration_rate_percent` | The rate every declared shipment is charged at, as a percentage of its insured amount. |
| `max_value_per_shipment` | The limit per shipment. A declared shipment above it is referred. |
| `valuation_basis` | What a declared shipment is insured for when it names no amount. `cif_plus_10` is the invoice value plus freight and other costs, plus 10%. |
| `estimated_annual_premium` | What the estimated annual value would cost at that rate, in SAR. |
| `deposit_premium` | The part of it paid up front to start the policy. |
| `premium`, `vat`, `total_price` | The deposit, its VAT, and what the customer pays now. |

## Issue the policy

The [Issue a policy API](/cargo-api-reference/policies/issue-a-policy) accepts the quote of an approved application:

```json
{
  "quote_request_id": "a34cf832-34e2-41a9-971f-46d2a0da3f57",
  "redirect_url": "https://partner.example.com/cargo/policies/policyID"
}
```

- `quote_request_id` is the application's `id`.
- `redirect_url` is where the customer is sent after paying. `policyID` anywhere in it is replaced with the policy's id.

The policy comes back **unpaid**: `status` is `0`, `coverage_confirmed` is `false` and `payment_link` is where the customer pays. Nothing is covered yet.

Calling Issue a policy again for the same application before it is paid returns the same policy, updated. Once it is paid, the call answers `40026` with that `policy_id`.

### Payment

Send the customer to the policy's `payment_link`. It opens Yasmina's secure card page, which shows the deposit, the VAT and the total.

- The link is valid for 24 hours. [Read the policy](/cargo-api-reference/policies/read-a-policy) to get the current one; it is renewed when it is about to expire.
- When the payment succeeds, the policy is issued and the customer is sent to your `redirect_url`.
- A failed or abandoned payment is never an API error. The policy stays unpaid until a payment succeeds.

### The issued policy

After payment, [Read the policy](/cargo-api-reference/policies/read-a-policy):

```json
{
  "id": 4,
  "status": 1,
  "coverage_confirmed": true,
  "payment_link": null,
  "price": 17250,
  "start_date": "2026-11-24",
  "provider_policy_id": "WAL/MOC/5015742",
  "policy_url": "https://sandbox.yasmina.ai/storage/generated-pdfs/cargo/policies/….pdf",
  "invoice_url": "https://sandbox.yasmina.ai/storage/generated-pdfs/cargo/invoices/….pdf",
  "purchased_at": "2026-11-24T08:42:48.000000Z",
  "meta_data": {
    "cover_start": "2026-11-24",
    "cover_end": "2027-11-23",
    "…": "the customer, the application and the quote the policy was issued from"
  },
  "declarations": { "count": 0, "insured_amount_sar": 0, "premium": 0 }
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `coverage_confirmed` | `true` once the policy is paid for and issued. **Tell the customer the cover is in place only when this is `true`.** |
| `provider_policy_id` | The insurance company's policy number. |
| `meta_data.cover_start`, `meta_data.cover_end` | The policy period: one year from the day the policy is issued. Both are `null` until then. |
| `policy_url` | The policy schedule (PDF). |
| `invoice_url` | The tax invoice (PDF). |
| `payment_link` | `null` once the policy is issued. |
| `declarations` | The shipments covered under the policy so far: how many, their total insured amount and their total premium, in SAR. |

The [List policies API](/cargo-api-reference/policies/list-policies) returns all your cargo policies, and filters by `customer_id`.

## Declare shipments

Once the policy is paid for and issued, the business declares each shipment with the [Declare a shipment API](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/declare-a-shipment) (`POST /policies/{id}/declarations`).

A declaration describes one shipment: the goods, the route, the value and the documents it travels under. It is covered straight away, so `departure_date`, `conveyance.carrier_name` and `references.invoice_number` are required, and `departure_date` has to fall within the policy period.

```json
{
  "shipment": {
    "goods": {
      "description": "400 cartons of tablets",
      "category": "electronics",
      "packaging": "container_fcl",
      "quantity": 400
    },
    "transport_mode": "sea",
    "origin": { "country": "CN", "city": "Shanghai" },
    "destination": { "country": "SA", "city": "Jeddah" },
    "invoice_value": 620000,
    "currency": "SAR",
    "freight": 14000,
    "departure_date": "2026-12-02",
    "conveyance": { "carrier_name": "Bahri Logistics", "vessel_name": "MV Jeddah Star" },
    "references": { "invoice_number": "INV-2026-0142", "bill_of_lading": "BHLU40112233" }
  }
}
```

**The insured amount.** Leave `insured_amount` out and the shipment is insured on the policy's `valuation_basis`: the invoice value plus `freight` and `other_costs`, plus 10%. Send `insured_amount` to insure a different amount. The invoice can be in any of the `currencies`; the premium is always in SAR.

The declaration's `status` says what happened:

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `issued` | The shipment is inside the policy's terms and is covered. `certificate_number` and `certificate_url` are its certificate. |
| `referred` | The shipment is outside the policy's terms: above the limit per shipment, a country or transport mode the policy was not written for, or hazardous goods. `reasons` says which. [Read the declaration](/cargo-api-reference/open-cover/read-a-declaration) again until the status changes. |
| `rejected` | The shipment cannot be covered. `reasons` says why. |

`premium` is what the shipment costs at the policy's `declaration_rate_percent`. Declared premiums are reported on each declaration and totalled on the policy under `declarations`; they are not paid through a payment link.

Declaring under a policy that is not paid for yet, or is past its last day, answers `40027`.

## Testing in Sandbox

In Sandbox the commercial registry and the insurance company are simulated, so you can reach every branch of the journey on purpose. Prices, exchange rates and documents are samples and carry no cover.

| To get | Send |
| --- | --- |
| A verified customer | Any 10-digit `cr_number` that does not end in `000`. The legal name and address are sample values. |
| `40031`, the registry has no record | A `cr_number` ending in `000`, without `legal_name` and `address`. |
| An approved application | An application with none of the conditions below. |
| A referred application | An `estimated_annual_value` above 100,000,000 SAR, a `max_value_per_shipment` above 5,000,000 SAR, or three or more entries in `claims_history`. |
| A rejected application | `KP` among `origin_countries` or `destination_countries`. |
| A referred declaration | A shipment above the policy's limit per shipment, an origin country, destination country or transport mode that is not on the policy, or `hazardous` among `goods.characteristics`. |
| A rejected declaration | `KP` as the origin or destination country, or `arms_and_ammunition` as the goods category. |

A referral is decided about 20 seconds after it was made: read the application or the declaration again after that and it is approved.

## Errors

| Endpoint | Code | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /cargo/customers` | `40031` | The commercial registry has no record of the CR number, and `legal_name` or `address` is missing. |
| `POST /cargo/policies` | `40032` | The application is referred or rejected. |
| | `40030` | The quote is past `valid_until`. Apply again. |
| | `40026` | The quote was already issued and paid for. `policy_id` is that policy. |
| `POST /cargo/policies/{id}/declarations` | `40027` | The policy is not paid for and issued, or is past its last day. |

A field that is missing or not valid answers `422` with code `42201`, and `details` names each field. The [Errors](/api-resources/errors#cargo-insurance) page has every code.
